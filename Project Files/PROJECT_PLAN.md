Project Plan – Monopoly Simulation
1. Project Objective

Develop a Java-based Monopoly movement simulator that estimates landing 
probabilities for all 40 board squares and compares two jail-exit strategies.

2. Project Scope

The simulation includes:

A standard 40-square Monopoly board.
Two six-sided dice.
Player movement and board wrapping.
Chance and Community Chest decks.
Get Out of Jail Free cards.
Consecutive doubles.
Go to Jail behavior.
Two jail-exit strategies.
Landing counters and probability calculations.

The simulation excludes money management, rent, auctions, property purchases, 
and interactions between multiple players.
